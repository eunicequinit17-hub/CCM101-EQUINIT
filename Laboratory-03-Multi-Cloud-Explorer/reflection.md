# Mission Reflection

This Multi-Cloud Explorer activity helped me understand the differences between AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform. Among the three cloud platforms, Google Cloud impressed me the most because of its strong support for Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, data analytics, and Kubernetes. I also learned that each provider has different strengths, so there is no single cloud platform that is always the best for every organization.

AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud have many similarities. All three provide virtual machines, object storage, networking, identity management, databases, and other cloud services. They also use global infrastructure with different regions and locations to support applications around the world. Because of these similarities, companies can choose a provider based on their specific technical and business requirements.

I learned that business requirements are very important when selecting a cloud platform. For example, a company using Microsoft products may benefit more from Azure, while an AI research company may prefer Google Cloud. A startup may prioritize cost, scalability, and easy access to different services. A global company may prioritize availability, automatic scaling, performance, and worldwide infrastructure.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant helped me think beyond simply choosing a cloud provider. I learned that technical decisions should be connected to the needs, budget, goals, and existing technologies of a business. I also learned how to compare cloud services and match them with real-world scenarios.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio also improved after completing this mission. I added research documents, comparison tables, client recommendations, Linux investigation results, and screenshots. This made my portfolio more organized and showed my progress in learning cloud computing. Overall, this activity gave me a better understanding of how cloud technologies can be selected and applied to different business situations.
